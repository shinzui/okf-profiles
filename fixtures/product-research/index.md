# Product Research Finding

- [Stakeholders ignore emailed attachments](attachments-ignored.md) - Several leads reported that stakeholders rarely open emailed result files.

- [Time to share dominates month-end pain](time-to-share-dominates.md) - Most interviewed finance leads named the hours between close and sharing as their main pain.
