# OKF Profile

- [terminology](profile.md) - Controlled project vocabulary with stable TERM handles: the canonical term, a one-sentence definition, accepted and discouraged wording, typed relations to other terms in this or another project, succession, and anchors to what embodies the term. A controlled vocabulary, not a general ontology: decisions, specifications, patterns, capabilities, and guides keep their own profiles.
