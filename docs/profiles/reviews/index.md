# OKF Profile

- [reviews](profile.md) - Records of an artifact having been reviewed: what was examined and under which stable identity, the commit it was examined at, who or which model examined it and at what effort, and what came of it. A finding worth acting on becomes a bug report or an improvement request; this corpus records the examination. A human who reads a model's review records that as an OKF `verified` entry under a `human:` actor, which is what `okf trust` reads to report the human-reviewed tier.
